# Section Questions (the five-field audit, by section type)

For each artifact section, the question we're really asking. The AI offers these options when the user can't name the question themselves — but the user picks. Never pick for them.

| Section type | The claim is | The question is |
|---|---|---|
| Hero / promise | "This promise is what people want" | Would anyone pick you for this — or could any business say it? |
| Problem | "This is what they actually feel" | Is this really what your customer feels — or what you think they feel? |
| Who this is for | "These are the people who come" | Is this the right person to write to — or everyone at once? |
| Plan / solution | "This is how the fix works" | Can you actually deliver this? Will they do what it takes? |
| Proof / testimonial / case | "Here's proof it works" | Is this real proof — or a hope? |
| Price / CTA | "People will pay for this" | Would someone actually pay for this? |
| FAQ / objections | "These are the real objections" | Are these the real reasons they hesitate — or the ones you assume? |

## The risk chains (which pin goes on which section)

- **Product risk** (orange) — the promises: hero, problem, plan/solution
- **Customer risk** (teal) — the people: who-this-is-for, proof/testimonial (the segment's voice)
- **Market risk** (purple) — the money and the alternatives: price/CTA, FAQ

## The anti-patterns (what each question guards against)

- Everyone-trap ("we serve everyone") → the Who-this-is-for question
- Feature dump (inside-out copy) → the Problem question
- Vague claims (no defensible difference) → the Hero question
- Promise without delivery (brand gap) → the Plan / Price questions
- Borrowed words (generic AI marketing-speak) → the Evidence field (quote the journey row, don't invent)
- Writing before knowing (copy before maps) → the Where-it-comes-from field (must trace)
- Jargon & abstraction → the customer's own words as the style guide

## The evidence statuses

- **Know** — real evidence exists: a client quote, a result, a number
- **Believe** — a claim, a hypothesis, no proof yet. Labeled "not sure yet — this is a hope."
- Everything starts as believe. It flips to know only when the user produces real evidence.
